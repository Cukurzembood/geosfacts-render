THE WINDOW THAT WATCHED BACK — test video (narrative cinematic, eerie mystery)
66 seconds, 30 fps, 1980 frames, 1080x1920. NO VO (silent narration track —
Gemini TTS quota is reserved for pipeline prep 052-056). The narration below
defines the shot timing; the story is carried by the visuals.
Original story — NOT the lift / floor-13 story from the Threads reference.

1.  0.00-6.00  The street was empty by midnight. Only one lamp still burned,
                and it flickered like it was afraid of the dark.
2.  6.00-12.00 Every window on the lane was black. Every window except one.
3. 12.00-18.00 He looked up. Someone was standing in the light.
                Then the light was empty.
4. 18.00-24.00 The lamp above him buzzed once, and died.
5. 24.00-30.00 Now there were two lights. Side by side. Like eyes.
6. 30.00-36.00 His book fell open to a page he had never written.
                Two words, in his own handwriting: don't knock.
7. 36.00-42.00 The front door stood open. Warm light spilled onto the
                street like an invitation.
8. 42.00-48.00 Inside, a clock ticked backwards. The hallway was longer
                than the house.
9. 48.00-54.00 The door slammed shut. No wind. No hand. Just shut.
10. 54.00-60.00 The lamp flickered back on. The street was the same as
                before. Almost.
11. 60.00-66.00 His book lay on the ground, pages turning by themselves.
                The house watched. The house always watched.
